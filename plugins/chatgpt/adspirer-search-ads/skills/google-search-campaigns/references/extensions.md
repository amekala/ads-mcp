# Google Ads extensions (assets)

Extensions add links, text and detail under a Search ad. They cost nothing extra and usually raise
click-through rate. Recommend them for every Search campaign, but only with real pages and real
facts.

## What to suggest

| Extension | Tool | Good starting point | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sitelinks | `add_sitelinks` | about 4 | Link text up to 25 characters, optional descriptions up to 35 each, and a real page per link. |
| Callouts | `add_callout_extensions` | 4–6 | Short, specific facts about the business, up to 25 characters. |
| Structured snippets | `add_structured_snippets` | 1 header | A real category (Services, Brands, Types) with 3–10 values. |

These counts are what tends to work, not requirements. If the site has two relevant pages, add two
sitelinks. **Never invent a landing page, price, guarantee, award or review** to fill a slot. Ask the
user, or leave it out.

`list_campaign_extensions` shows what's already there. Don't duplicate.

## Writing them well

- **Sitelinks shouldn't repeat the ad.** Point them at the next thing a ready buyer wants: pricing,
  service areas, reviews, booking. Each must land on a page that exists and matches its label.
- **Callouts are facts, not slogans.** "Same-day appointments" helps; "Quality you can trust" doesn't.
- **Snippets need a real category.** Don't mix unrelated items under one header.

## Removal is permanent

`remove_sitelinks`, `remove_callouts` and `remove_structured_snippets` delete the asset, and its
history goes with it. Confirm before removing, and prefer replacing.

## Also consider

Call, location and price extensions matter for local and e-commerce advertisers. Suggest them when
the business has a phone number, a storefront or published prices.
